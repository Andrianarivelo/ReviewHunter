ReviewHunter Connector

Save references and PDFs from your browser into your local library.

Connection

The desktop app listens on http://127.0.0.1:8765 by default. Change it only if you started ReviewHunter on another port.

Saving

Used by context-menu saves. The popup remembers the collection you used last.

Keyboard shortcut

Open the popup with Ctrl + Shift + Y (Cmd + Shift + Y on macOS). Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
